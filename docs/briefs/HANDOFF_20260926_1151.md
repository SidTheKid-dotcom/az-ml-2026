## Problem and score

- Source 1 is a clean deduplicated business list. Sources 2 and 3 contain noisy
  copies, look-alikes, and records belonging to no Source-1 business.
- For each Source-1 business, predict its matching Source-2/3 IDs.
- For nonempty truth, per-business
  `F0.5 = 5 * TP / (4 * predicted_count + true_count)`.
- A true singleton with an empty prediction scores 1; a singleton with any
  predicted ID scores 0. The official score averages over every Source-1 row.
- Precision is expensive: adding a link generally needs about 0.73–0.79
  correctness to break even, depending on list length.
- Train: 2,206,821 S1 and 10,320,219 S2/S3 records, labeled, US and India.
- Test: 1,732,544 S1 and 9,969,589 S2/S3 records, including 259,452 French S1.
  France has no supplied labels.
- Every matched S2/S3 record belongs to at most one S1 in train. The final file
  must therefore contain no contested record ID.
- About 5.6% of train S1s have no match. True list length has mean 3.46 and
  maximum 11.
- Test is denser than train: about 5.75 target records per S1 versus 4.68.
- Use only supplied business records. No external lookup, geocoding, task labels,
  or downloaded data. Licensed model families must stay under the 8B total
  parameter cap.
- One leaderboard increment of 0.001 is about 1,733 full-test entity-points.
